<H1>MY 3D ART SHOWCASE</H1>
Hey everyone I am Vex and I have been making 3d art for quite a while even though I may not have many good arts to show, I thought it will be a good idea to add a place where I can upload and showcase my art.
For that purpose I made this website where I can showcase my art!

![Website preview](files/Screenshot%202026-10-07%20022906.png)

This website was made using HTML and CSS couple of weeks ago I made a personal website I remember it got messy real quick but this time it was smooth and it was pretty easy to make.
I thought it will take more than 5 hours to make but DAYUM I made it in ~2hours.

To make the website look good I added a hovering thingy.....I'm not sure what it is called but when you hover over an art the image gets slightly big and it shows small shows beside it and it look very good.

I did not use too many colours in this website as I often like minimal things.

The font I used is Isometra by Ben Dunkle.
You can check it out here: [ISOMETRA](https://fonts.google.com/specimen/Isometra?preview.script=Latn)
